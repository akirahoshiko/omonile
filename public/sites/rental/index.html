Good to know

Questions, answered

Anything else? Ask us directly.

How much does a stay cost?

Nightly rates run from $95 / night (Forest Deck Studio) to $380 / night (Sunset Pool House). A one-off cleaning fee is added per stay. There are no service fees when you book direct.

How do I book?

Choose a stay, pick your dates and send an enquiry. Your host confirms availability and sends a secure payment link. (This template does not take real bookings.)

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. After that, the first night (at the nightly rate shown) is non-refundable.

Is a deposit required?

A 30% deposit secures your dates, with the balance due 14 days before arrival. Stays of 7+ nights get a lower nightly rate: ask us for a quote.

What time is check-in?

Self check-in with a smart lock is available at every home from 15:00. Check-out is 11:00.

Are pets welcome?

Selected stays accept well-behaved pets for a small flat fee. Mention it in your enquiry and your host will confirm.
